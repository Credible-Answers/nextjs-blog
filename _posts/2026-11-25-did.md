Differences in Differences: our first blog post.

Consider groups $g$ observed over periods $t$, with outcome $Y_{g,t}$ and treatment $D_{g,t}$. The classic two-way fixed effects (TWFE) regression is

$$
Y_{g,t} = \alpha_g + \gamma_t + \beta_{fe} D_{g,t} + \varepsilon_{g,t}.
$$

de Chaisemartin and D'Haultfœuille (2020) show that $\beta_{fe}$ is a weighted sum of the treatment effects $\Delta_{g,t}$ of the treated cells:

$$
\beta_{fe} = \sum_{(g,t):\, D_{g,t}=1} w_{g,t}\, \Delta_{g,t}, \qquad \sum_{(g,t):\, D_{g,t}=1} w_{g,t} = 1.
$$

**DID you know that** some weights $w_{g,t}$ can be negative? Then $\beta_{fe}$ may be negative even if every $\Delta_{g,t} > 0$.
